\title{ARBOR: Structural Guarantees for LLM-Extracted Narrative Hierarchies via Maximum Weight Arborescence}

\author{Anonymous ARR submission}

\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{ARBOR}\footnote{ARBOR: Latin \emph{arbor} (``tree''), after the \emph{arborescence} at the core of our method.}, an open-source system that extracts structured narrative knowledge from Chinese long-form fiction and produces four interactive views (relationship graph, procedural world map, timeline, character encyclopedia). Per-chapter LLM extraction at novel scale produces per-node assignments that are mostly correct, yet the aggregate hierarchy still \textbf{collapses structurally}: greedy vote aggregation accumulates up to 203 children under a single parent, produces cycles, and fragments single locations into duplicate nodes across surface variants. We argue that what long-document extraction needs is not higher per-node accuracy, but \textbf{structural guarantees} that greedy aggregation cannot provide. We pair per-chapter LLM extraction with an \textbf{Edmonds-augmented hybrid algorithm} that preserves reliable LLM-local parent judgments and applies Chu-Liu/Edmonds maximum-weight arborescence to fill structural gaps ($\sim$130\,ms, no LLM dependency); complement it with a composable \textsc{SuffixNormalizer} skill merging 60+ surface variants on the most alias-rich novel; and report three-layer symbolic validation. On a benchmark of five Ming--Qing classical vernacular novels (4{,}941 expert-reviewed nodes), ARBOR's pipeline \textbf{preserves per-node accuracy} relative to greedy voting on the shared node set (fair comparison: 0.9101 vs.\ 0.9062 average Overall, per-novel differences within $\pm$0.01) while delivering qualitatively different structural guarantees: single-root acyclic trees, max fan-out reduced from 203 to 57 on the most severe case (72\% reduction), and 60+ variant merges on the most affected novel. An additional LLM single-shot CoT baseline, given the same vote evidence Edmonds sees plus pretrained knowledge, also fails --- dropping 1--6 input locations, hallucinating 3--10, with unstable fan-out (74/143). These guarantees are algorithmic properties of the aggregation layer, not artifacts of prompt engineering. We release the pipeline, the 4{,}941-node gold, and the evaluation harness at \url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/arbor-x9k3a}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Long-form fiction analysis --- extracting characters, relationships, locations, and events into queryable knowledge --- has traditionally required weeks of manual scholarly effort. LLM-based information extraction \cite{wei2024zeroshot,wadhwa2023revisiting} succeeds at passage scale but exposes qualitatively new failure modes when scaled to novel length (100--2{,}500 chapters, 0.5--8M characters). Pretrained LLMs can recognize \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}孙悟空\end{CJK} or \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}贾宝玉\end{CJK} in isolation, yet the \textit{aggregation} of their per-chapter outputs into a coherent narrative knowledge graph fails along four dimensions:

\textbf{(i) Entity drift.} Generic terms (\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}土地\end{CJK} earth god, \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}小妖\end{CJK} minor demon) are surfaced as named characters, though each refers to different individuals per chapter.
\textbf{(ii) Historical semantic ambiguity.} The suffix \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}州\end{CJK} denotes a province in \textit{Three Kingdoms} but a prefecture in \textit{Water Margin} (Song dynasty); no existing system adapts to narrative era.
\textbf{(iii) Spatial inversion.} Containment direction is frequently reversed, e.g.\ \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}水帘洞\end{CJK} (Water Curtain Cave) \textit{contains} \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}花果山\end{CJK} (Flower Fruit Mountain); 30.8\% of extracted containment relations in \textit{Dream of the Red Chamber} had incorrect direction.
\textbf{(iv) Structural collapse.} Per-node parent voting accumulates 203 children under a single catch-all parent in \textit{Journey to the West}; cycles emerge; same-location surface variants (\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}乌斯藏/乌斯藏国/乌斯藏国界\end{CJK}) fragment into spurious duplicate nodes.

The four dimensions follow the logic of knowledge-graph construction itself: entities surfaced and resolved (i), names interpreted against historical semantics (ii), spatial containment organized (iii), and the aggregate assembled (iv). We argue that (i)--(iv) constitute a \textbf{different category} of extraction failure than those addressed by standard IE benchmarks. Per-node accuracy metrics fail to penalize an aggregate graph with 203 children under one node or with cycles; what is needed is \textbf{structural guarantees on the aggregate} --- single root, acyclicity, reduced fan-out, merged variants --- that greedy aggregation cannot provide.

\noindent\textbf{Contributions.} (1) A \textbf{hybrid LLM-base + Edmonds-augmented aggregation pipeline} \cite{chu1965,edmonds1967} for narrative location hierarchy from noisy per-chapter LLM extractions: it preserves reliable per-chapter LLM parent judgments while using maximum-weight arborescence on residual orphans plus post-pass structural repair, completing aggregation in 130\,ms; the hybrid design empirically outperforms pure MWA on this data (\autoref{tab:pure-mwa-ablation}), reduces max fan-out from 203 (greedy voting) to 57 (72\%) with provable acyclicity and single-rootedness, and parallels non-projective dependency parsing \cite{mcdonald2005}. (2) \textsc{SuffixNormalizer}, a composable post-Edmonds skill merging Chinese location surface variants (60+ merges per novel in \textit{Journey} alone), and \textbf{dynasty-aware place-name classification}. (3) A \textbf{fair per-node ablation} on the intersection of nodes produced by both pipelines: our full pipeline matches greedy voting within $\pm$0.01 Overall on all five novels (5-novel avg 0.9101 vs.\ 0.9062), demonstrating that the structural improvements come at no accuracy cost. (4) \textbf{LLM aggregation baselines} (per-chapter zero-shot and single-shot CoT, Claude Sonnet 4) that receive the same vote evidence Edmonds sees; both exhibit the structural failure modes guarantees prevent, including pretraining knowledge of one novel leaking into another's hierarchy. (5) A \textbf{5-novel errata benchmark} (4{,}941 expert-reviewed nodes, five dimensions, partially validated IAA) and an \textbf{open-source end-to-end pipeline} (four narrative-knowledge views) with an evaluation harness that regenerates every tabular claim in this paper from the released JSON.

\section{System}
\label{sec:system}

ARBOR processes a novel through four stages: chapter splitting (adaptive regex over 15+ heading patterns) and entity pre-scanning (jieba + LLM classification); per-chapter LLM extraction with a Scene-Graph Chain-of-Thought spatial-reasoning guide; cross-chapter validation (\S\ref{sec:validation}); and visualization (Appendix~\ref{app:views}). Main extraction uses temperature 0.1; relation-subtype classification uses temperature 0.7 with majority-of-3 self-consistency voting; other decoding parameters follow provider defaults.

\subsection{Three-Layer Validation}
\label{sec:validation}

We organize post-extraction validation into three layers of increasing cost.
\textbf{Layer 1: structural features.} Rule-based checks on graph structure: phantom-parent detection (low-mention hubs), zero-evidence node flagging, tier-inversion detection, disambiguation prefix co-existence.

\textbf{Layer 2: domain knowledge.} Morphological suffix ranking (110+ suffixes $\rightarrow$ 7 ranks) reverses rank-violating containment; dynasty-aware classification determines \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}州\end{CJK} as kingdom or city by era; curated prefix/verb lists disambiguate \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}府\end{CJK}/\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}处\end{CJK}.

\textbf{Layer 3: text verification.} Direct substring search in the full novel ($<$0.5\,s per novel) with a \texttt{TextVerifier.context()} returning 5 windowed snippets per occurrence. Nodes with mc$=$0 but text presence reveal LLM recall gaps; we found 392 such cases across \textit{Journey} and \textit{Red Chamber}.

\subsection{Global Hierarchy as Edmonds-Augmented LLM Base Assignment}
\label{sec:hierarchy}

\paragraph{Problem formulation.}
We model location hierarchy as a constrained arborescence induction problem
over a candidate edge graph. Let $V$ be the set of unique locations observed
across all chapter extractions, augmented with a virtual uber-root\footnote{The uber-root (\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}天下\end{CJK}) is an engineering container, not a humanities-concept claim: \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}天下\end{CJK} (Huaxia-centric politico-cultural order), \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}世界\end{CJK} (Buddhist-introduced spatio-temporal notion), and \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}主世界\end{CJK} (``overworld'' in game/web-novel usage) have distinct concept histories; the system treats them uniformly without claiming conceptual analysis.}
$r \notin V_{\text{obs}}$. Let $E \subseteq V \times V$ be the set of candidate
parent--child edges (a directed edge $u \rightarrow v$ encodes ``$u$ is a
candidate parent of $v$''), and let $w(u \rightarrow v) \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$
be the edge weight. An \emph{arborescence} rooted at $r$ is a set of edges
$T \subseteq E$ such that every $v \in V \setminus \{r\}$ has exactly one
incoming edge in $T$ and no cycles exist. The unconstrained maximum-weight
arborescence is
\begin{equation}
T^*_{\text{MWA}} = \arg\max_{T \in \mathcal{T}(V, r)} \sum_{(u \rightarrow v) \in T} w(u \rightarrow v),
\label{eq:mwa}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{T}(V, r)$ is the set of all valid arborescences. However,
direct optimization of \autoref{eq:mwa} is \emph{not} the algorithm we deploy:
pure MWA on $(V, E, w)$ overrides reliable per-chapter LLM parent judgments
whenever the global weight sum favors a different assignment, and provides
no fan-out control (\autoref{tab:pure-mwa-ablation}).

\paragraph{Algorithm: LLM-base + Edmonds-augmented repair.}
The deployed pipeline (\autoref{alg:edmonds-hybrid}) preserves LLM-extracted
parents as a base assignment and applies Chu-Liu/Edmonds
\cite{chu1965,edmonds1967} surgically to fill structural gaps:

\begin{algorithm}[h]
\caption{Edmonds-Augmented Hierarchy Resolution}
\label{alg:edmonds-hybrid}
\scriptsize
\textbf{Input:} candidate graph $G = (V \cup \{r\}, E, w)$, LLM-extracted base
parents $B: V \rightarrow V \cup \{\bot\}$ \\
\textbf{Parameters:} prior-override threshold $\tau = 15.0$, target fan-out
$K = 30$ (best-effort)
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Base + targeted overrides.}\quad
  $P \leftarrow B$. For each $v \in V$: if $\exists u$ with
  $\text{prefix}(u) \subset \text{prefix}(v)$ (Chinese
  name-containment heuristic), set $P[v] \leftarrow u$. Then
  for each $v$, if $\exists u$ with $w(u \rightarrow v) \geq \tau$
  (high-confidence prior or vote), set $P[v] \leftarrow u$.
\item \textbf{Edmonds on orphans.}\quad
  Solve \autoref{eq:mwa} via Chu-Liu/Edmonds on the full graph $G$ to obtain
  $T^*$. For each $v \in V$ with $P[v] = \bot$: set $P[v] \leftarrow$
  parent of $v$ in $T^*$.
\item \textbf{Cycle repair.}\quad
  Detect cycles in $P$ via DFS. For each cycle, break the weakest edge
  $(u_{\min} \rightarrow v_{\min})$ and reassign $P[v_{\min}]$ to its
  next-best-weighted parent.
\item \textbf{Phantom-parent lift.}\quad
  For each $u$ with mention-count $\leq 3$ and $|\text{children}(u)| \geq 5$
  and $|\text{children}(u)| / \max(\text{mc}(u), 1) \geq 3$ (phantom
  catch-all detection): lift weakest children
  (mention-count $\leq 1$) to $u$'s parent until $|\text{children}(u)| \leq 3$.
\item \textbf{Degree balancing (best-effort).}\quad
  Reassign children of any $u$ with $|\text{children}(u)| > K$
  to larger-tier siblings with spare capacity, while absorbers remain.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Output:} parent assignment $P: V \rightarrow V \cup \{r\}$
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Why hybrid, not pure MWA.}
Three design forces motivate the hybrid over direct \autoref{eq:mwa}:
\textbf{(i) LLM local judgment is reliable} (68--81\% per-chapter parent
accuracy on the 5-novel gold); pure MWA discards this signal whenever global
weights favor a different assignment.
\textbf{(ii) Edmonds-only output lacks fan-out control.} A fan-out
target ($K = 30$) keeps downstream visualization layout stable; encoding it
inside \autoref{eq:mwa} would make optimization NP-hard, so it is applied
as a best-effort post-pass.
\textbf{(iii) Cycle prevention is cheaper post-hoc} than as an optimization constraint ($O(n)$ DFS repair).
Step 2 dominates the runtime: $O(mn)$ via textbook Chu-Liu/Edmonds (NetworkX's
\texttt{maximum\_spanning\_arborescence}, verified on a 1000-graph random
harness); on the 5-novel corpus ($n \leq 1{,}700$, $m \leq 8{,}000$) the full
hybrid completes in 83--131\,ms per novel.

\paragraph{Edge weight sources.}
$w(u \rightarrow v)$ accumulates from four additive channels:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Per-chapter votes}: each chapter extraction $u \rightarrow v$ contributes $1 \cdot \text{decay}(t)$, $\text{decay}(t) = 1.0 + 0.5 \cdot (t / T_{\max})$ slightly favoring later chapters.
\item \textbf{Spatial-relation extractions}: ``contains'' relations with rank-validated direction contribute $+0.5$ to $+2.0$ weighted by LLM confidence.
\item \textbf{Name-containment} ($+25$): if $v$'s surface form is a substring of $u$'s (e.g.\ \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}梁山泊\end{CJK} $\supset$ \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}梁山\end{CJK}).
\item \textbf{Knowledge priors} ($+20$): 399 curated edges across three novels grounding well-known geographic facts.
\end{itemize}
Name-containment and priors thus dominate vote evidence wherever they apply, encoding our design prior that they are more reliable than noisy co-occurrence aggregation.

\paragraph{What the algorithm provides vs.\ what preprocessing provides.}
We distinguish properties guaranteed by the algorithm from those enforced by pre/post-processing:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Acyclicity} (Steps 3--5): repair-and-verify --- cycle breaking iterates to a fixpoint (each round rewires one cycle's weakest edge and creates none); since Steps 4--5 may reintroduce cycles, the same fixpoint pass runs once more after them. The output is acyclic on every input.
\item \textbf{Connectedness to root} (preprocessing): fallback edges $(r, v)$ with weight $\epsilon$ are injected for each $v$ lacking incoming edges, so Edmonds always returns an arborescence; single-rootedness arises from this augmentation, not from the Edmonds objective.
\item \textbf{Fan-out reduction} (Step 5): best-effort post-pass redistribution onto larger-tier siblings with spare capacity --- an engineering objective orthogonal to the optimization; on flat geographies residual fan-out $> K$ can remain.
\end{itemize}

The hard properties hold on every input; fan-out reduction is best-effort (72\% on the worst case, \autoref{tab:ablation}). Greedy voting and LLM-CoT baselines lack mechanisms for any of them (§\ref{sec:llm-baselines}); the hybrid delivers them regardless of extraction quality.

\paragraph{Composable skill pipeline.}
The pipeline is implemented as composable \textbf{Geographic Agent Skills}
(\textsc{TierClassifier}, \textsc{VoteBuilder}, \textsc{KnowledgePrior},
\textsc{EdmondsResolver}, \textsc{SuffixNormalizer}), each consuming an
immutable snapshot and producing a delta; the version chain enables
rollback, skill-subset ablation (\autoref{tab:pure-mwa-ablation}), and
bit-identical re-analysis of frozen extractions (same-input double runs
differ by zero edges).
\textsc{SuffixNormalizer} (run after \autoref{alg:edmonds-hybrid}) applies
a two-pass approach: an explicit synonym table curated per novel
(\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}乌斯藏\end{CJK} $\equiv$
\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}乌斯藏国\end{CJK} $\equiv$
\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}乌斯藏国界\end{CJK}), plus a suffix-stripping pass
that merges variants whose stripped form exists as another node. This
reduces 60+ spurious nodes in \textit{Journey} alone while preserving
arborescence validity.

\subsection{Fair Per-Node Baseline: Voting vs.\ Pipeline}
\label{sec:fair-baseline}

A naive comparison of our pipeline against per-chapter majority voting is confounded: voting produces fewer nodes than the full pipeline (which adds synthetic parents via TierClassifier, merges variants via SuffixNormalizer, and inserts priors), and gold-flagged error nodes \textit{absent} from voting's output count as ``resolved'' --- up to 48\% / 31\% artificial score boost on \textit{Red Chamber} / \textit{Three Kingdoms}. To correct for this, we evaluate both systems \textbf{on the intersection of nodes they both produce} (Table~\ref{tab:fair}). Per-novel differences between the full pipeline and voting are all within $\pm$0.01 Overall (averages 0.9101 / 0.9062, +0.39\,pp) --- statistically indistinguishable. On the frozen v0.71 snapshot, the pooled full-pipeline Overall has bootstrap 95\% CI [0.9213, 0.9354] (10{,}000 novel-level replications), and re-extraction across temperatures 0.0--1.0 shifts per-node metrics by std $\leq 0.2$\,pp. This small gap is not our main claim; rather, Table~\ref{tab:fair} establishes that the structural guarantees of \S\ref{sec:structural} come \textbf{at no accuracy cost}.

\subsection{Structural Guarantees (Primary Contribution)}
\label{sec:structural}

Table~\ref{tab:ablation} reports the structural properties where our pipeline departs qualitatively from greedy voting (release v0.78, pinned pure-extraction start state). On the pathological case (\textit{Journey to the West}), voting produces a tree whose root has \textbf{203 children} --- a flat catch-all accumulating all unanchored locations; the full pipeline (Edmonds + priors + SuffixNormalizer) cuts this to 57, a 72\% reduction (the frozen v0.71 snapshot read 279 $\rightarrow$ 63, 77\%). On a 5-novel average, the full pipeline's max\_children is 74, versus voting's 143; on the frozen snapshot voting additionally left an average of 25 disjoint roots per novel and cycles on 2 of 5 novels (\textit{Dream of the Red Chamber}, \textit{Three Kingdoms}). Beyond fan-out, the hybrid pipeline yields single-root acyclic trees \textbf{by construction} (cycle-repair plus uber-root fallback augmentation, \autoref{alg:edmonds-hybrid}); voting provides no such property. Priors contribute structurally near-neutrally on \textit{Journey} (64 $\rightarrow$ 65 $\rightarrow$ 63 max\_ch on the frozen snapshot; \S\ref{sec:priors}). A no-Edmonds ablation (voting + repair passes only, orphans forced to the uber-root; frozen snapshot) still yields a valid tree with max\_ch 80: most fan-out reduction comes from the post-passes, while Edmonds' distinct contribution is global-optimal placement of the 283 vote-less orphans that voting leaves as disjoint roots.

\label{sec:cross-genre}

Eight additional novels without gold (Table~\ref{tab:cross}, Appendix~\ref{app:cross}) transfer without retuning: the xianxia serial \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}凡人修仙传\end{CJK} (2{,}451 ch.) and steampunk-occult \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}诡秘之主\end{CJK} (1{,}395 ch.) cover both Chinese-fantasy subgenres; \textit{The Lord of the Rings} is an outlier at 137 --- a \textit{naming-morphology}, not \textit{genre}, boundary of our Chinese-specific SuffixNormalizer. \subsection{LLM Aggregation Baselines}
\label{sec:llm-baselines}

A natural question is whether the collapse of per-chapter voting (\S\ref{sec:structural}) is specific to \textit{voting}, or whether a capable LLM --- per-chapter zero-shot, or given the full vote evidence in one chain-of-thought call --- aggregates better (Claude Sonnet 4, matching the main pipeline's LLM to isolate the aggregation variable; Table~\ref{tab:llm-baselines}).
Two findings. \textbf{(a) Structural inconsistency.} LLM-CoT --- given the same location set and per-location parent-candidate counts Edmonds sees, plus pretraining knowledge of the two novels --- produces trees with max fan-out 74/143 (spread 69); Edmonds + priors + SuffixNormalizer stays at 63/76 (spread 13).\footnote{Reference values 63/76: frozen v0.71 evaluation DB; pure-start v0.78 values in \autoref{tab:ablation}.} On \textit{Red Chamber} specifically, LLM-CoT collapses into 6 disjoint roots, defeating single-rootedness. \textbf{(b) Data corruption.} LLM-CoT drops 6 / 1 input locations (\textit{Journey} / \textit{Red Chamber}) and hallucinates 10 / 3 names not in the input; a particularly striking failure places \textit{Journey to the West}'s four continents (\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}东胜神洲/西牛贺洲/南赡部洲/北俱芦洲\end{CJK}) as top-level roots in the \textit{Red Chamber} hierarchy --- pretraining contamination as hallucination source. Zero-shot per-chapter aggregation is worse still: 101 disjoint roots on \textit{Journey}, 56 on \textit{Red Chamber}, parent\_P degrading to 62.2\% / 48.8\%. \textbf{The GraphRAG-style community-detection baseline} --- hierarchical Louvain on the entity co-occurrence graph with the same extraction as full pipeline --- fails differently: it collapses \textit{Red Chamber} into 3 disjoint top-level communities and \textit{Journey} into 7, because community structure captures \emph{co-occurrence affinity} rather than \emph{part-whole containment} (parent precision 19.6\% / 11.1\%). These baselines establish that the structural guarantees reported in \S\ref{sec:structural} are algorithmic properties unavailable from prompt engineering alone. A cross-LLM check reproduces the pattern with a different extractor: on a DeepSeek V3 extraction of \textit{Journey} (100 chapters, 537 locations), voting again yields an invalid tree (5 disjoint roots, max\_ch 56) while the full pipeline returns a valid one (single root, max\_ch 46)\footnote{Input extractions not archived; these numbers are contemporary experimental records, not re-verifiable with the released harness.} --- the failure mode belongs to the aggregation algorithm, not to one LLM.

\paragraph{Where priors matter.}
\label{sec:priors}
Ablating the KnowledgePrior skill (399 curated entries) shows it contributes \textbf{effectively zero to per-node Overall} on the 5-novel gold (average delta $-0.0003$), but drives \textbf{canonical-name correctness} (alias-group accuracy on the ten largest groups: 52.2\% $\rightarrow$ 100\% on \textit{Journey}/\textit{Red Chamber}). Hand-curated priors are worth the labor for specific failure modes but do not generalize across all dimensions.

\subsection{Contamination-Free Validation}
\label{sec:contamination-free}

A natural objection to Table~\ref{tab:main} is that Claude \emph{knows} all five novels (\S\ref{sec:eval}). To isolate structuring ability from memorization, we generated a fully synthetic 29-chapter xianxia novel \textit{Star-Dust Calamity} (\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}星尘劫\end{CJK}, 95K CJK characters) with DeepSeek V3, with all 42 locations, 15 characters, and 23 relations pre-specified in a ground-truth bible. It postdates all training cutoffs; forbidden-name scanning finds zero leakage, and generator and extractor are different LLMs.

Running the full pipeline on this unseen corpus (Table~\ref{tab:contamination}, Appendix~\ref{app:contamination}) yields \textbf{Overall = 0.751}, with the $\sim$0.15 gap vs seen novels concentrated in parent precision. The \textbf{structural guarantees hold completely}: single root, acyclic, max\_children = 10 (tighter than any seen novel). Character alias resolution is perfect on this 15-character cast (real-novel alias under-merging is discussed in Limitations); location recall is 97.6\% (41/42). The parent-precision gap is driven largely by a semantic tie --- our pipeline defaults to the generic uber-root \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}天下\end{CJK} while the bible uses the novel-specific \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}九曜天\end{CJK}. \textbf{This is consistent with the pipeline's structural contribution being orthogonal to LLM pretraining}: on unseen text the aggregation still produces a valid tree with extraction recall above 97\%.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Why structural guarantees matter beyond benchmark scores.} Our headline contribution is not higher per-node accuracy but \textit{unconditional structural properties}: downstream consumers of a narrative knowledge graph --- visualization layout, graph-traversal queries --- require well-formed tree inputs by contract. A 203-sibling catch-all (greedy voting, §\ref{sec:structural}) makes force-directed layout unstable; cycles break transitive queries; multi-root outputs fragment character encyclopedias. We do not empirically validate downstream-task performance.
\textbf{When MWA is not applicable.} Our formulation assumes (i) every non-root location has exactly one parent (tree, not DAG), and (ii) the narrative has a single top-level world. (i) breaks for overlapping jurisdictions; (ii) for multi-world narratives (\S\ref{sec:cross-genre}). The underlying \emph{LLM-as-extractor, algorithm-as-aggregator} pattern --- noisy local decisions to the LLM, global combinatorial decisions to an algorithm with formal guarantees --- transfers wherever per-document extractions must be reconciled into a well-formed global structure.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Narrative and book-scale IE.} BookNLP \cite{bamman2014bayesian} pioneered character identification and quote attribution for English fiction but does not address Chinese honorifics, fictional geography, or cross-chapter entity resolution \cite{bagga1998entity,cattan2021crossdoc}. LiSCU \cite{brahman2021plot} targets character-centric narrative understanding; Labatut and Bost \cite{labatut2019fictional} survey fictional character network extraction. NarrativeQA, NovelQA, and BookSum \cite{kocisky2018narrativeqa,wang2025novelqa,kryscinski2022booksum} benchmark comprehension and summarization on full books. None of these works produce a hierarchical geography-of-the-story artifact nor address the distinctive challenges of classical vernacular novels: morphological rank cues in place names (\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}界/洲/国/城/谷/洞/殿\end{CJK}) and cross-chapter entity drift at the 100--2{,}500-chapter scale.

\textbf{LLM-based information extraction.} Recent LLM-based IE \cite{wei2024zeroshot,wadhwa2023revisiting} succeeds at passage scale; GoLLIE \cite{sainz2024gollie} and UniversalNER \cite{zhou2024universalner} improve zero-shot extraction via guideline-aware schemas and targeted distillation. These works establish single-document competence, not cross-chapter aggregation, where the qualitative failure modes emerge (\S\ref{sec:structural}, \S\ref{sec:llm-baselines}). In terms of extraction scope, open-domain RE discovers relation types freely, while restricted-domain work pre-specifies a schema --- e.g.\ restricted-domain RE over ancient Chinese books \cite{liu2025guji} and dialogue-based character-relation extraction from novels \cite{yan2025novelre}; GenWebNovel \cite{zhao2025genwebnovel} documents the genre-adaptation difficulty of entity annotation on Chinese web novels. Our setting is a hybrid: entities are pre-specified (locations and characters), while containment relations are discovered openly from per-chapter evidence.

\textbf{Taxonomy induction via spanning trees.} MST- and arborescence-based hierarchy induction has a long lineage \cite{kozareva2010taxonomy,velardi2013ontolearn,bansal2014taxonomy}. Crucially, these works induce \textit{hypernym} (is-a) taxonomies from lexical patterns \cite{hearst1992hyponyms} or distributional statistics. We transfer the arborescence-optimization methodology to a substantively different setting: \textit{part-whole} hierarchies over fictional geography, induced from noisy per-chapter LLM extractions, with a Chinese morphological suffix rank (110+ suffixes, 7 tiers) as a prior unavailable in general taxonomy induction. McDonald et al.\ \cite{mcdonald2005} first applied Chu-Liu/Edmonds to non-projective dependency parsing; we establish an analogous formulation at a coarser unit (locations as nodes, weighted candidate-parent edges, optimal arborescence as best-supported containment hierarchy).

\textbf{Graph construction and long-context alternatives.} GraphRAG \cite{edge2024graphrag} builds entity--relation graphs via LLM community summarization; applied to our extraction, its algorithmic core yields parent precision below 20\% (\S\ref{sec:llm-baselines}). Long-context models \cite{liu2023lost} ingest whole novels but degrade mid-context.
\textbf{Spatial and Chinese NLP.} SpatialEval \cite{yamada2024spatial} documents LLM spatial-reasoning weaknesses at the sentence level; we extend this to narrative-scale containment inference (\S1 (iii)). Chinese NER \cite{zhang2018chinesener} and RE \cite{lin2019fewrel} target newswire and few-shot settings; neither addresses classical-fiction phenomena such as alias systems or fictional place names.

\textbf{Geoparsing and spatial humanities.} Geoparsing \cite{gritta2018geoparsing} grounds location mentions in real-world gazetteers; fictional geography has no external referent and must be induced from the narrative itself. Literary cartography \cite{moretti1998atlas,piatti2011cartographies,bodenhamer2010spatial} maps fictional space manually; we automate its structural layer; CHGIS/CBDB \cite{chgis,cbdb} motivate our knowledge priors.

\section*{Limitations}

\textbf{Annotator strictness gap.} The three-annotator IAA study (\S\ref{sec:eval}) shows the first-author errata applied a looser ``valid location'' criterion than the strictest of the three annotators: generic references like \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}天下\end{CJK} or \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}梦中花园\end{CJK} are retained by the first author but flagged as non-specific under that stricter reading. Structural guarantees (\S\ref{sec:structural}) are \emph{invariant} to this gap by construction, but per-node scores (Table~\ref{tab:main}) should be read with a $\pm$3--5pp confidence band pending guideline refinement.
\textbf{Unevaluated components.} Character alias resolution and fine-grained relation classification are supporting components of the pipeline but are \emph{not evaluated against gold standards} in this paper. Internal spot checks surface two recurring issues: (i) a non-trivial share of main-character alias groups remain under-merged across chapters (e.g., \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}南海观世音\end{CJK} / \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}观音菩萨\end{CJK} in \textit{Journey to the West}); (ii) the 6-category relation taxonomy exhibits boundary ambiguity for culturally-specific Chinese relations (e.g., \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}结拜兄弟\end{CJK} sworn brotherhood, \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}师兄弟\end{CJK} fellow-disciple), whose placement among \emph{intimate} / \emph{social} / \emph{hierarchical} is a taxonomy-design choice on which annotators disagree, and which also manifests as weak type-level classification on \textit{Water Margin}. Neither issue affects the structural guarantees reported in \S\ref{sec:structural}.
\textbf{Textual versions.} \textit{Dream of the Red Chamber} circulates in multiple recensions (Chengjia/Chengyi print editions, Gengchen manuscript); our benchmark uses a single electronic edition (the 122-chapter segmented source, Table~\ref{tab:data}), and entity or location differences induced by textual variants are unmodeled. Treating recensions as multiple evidence sources within the same vote-and-arborescence framework is a natural extension we leave to future work.
\textbf{Homonymous place names.} Historical Chinese geography reuses toponyms across regions, so we separate two disambiguation layers: \emph{intra-textual} (a node's position is decided by contextual evidence within the novel itself) and \emph{external linking} to real-world gazetteers. A case surfaced by external expert review of the released errata illustrates the boundary: for \textit{Water Margin}'s \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}盖州\end{CJK} (Gaizhou), the intra-textual layer attaches the node under the Hedong/Shanxi-side campaign geography described by the novel, while the external layer's default gazetteer candidate --- the modern Gaizhou in Liaoning, a different historical place --- was intercepted by our validation gate. External linking thus requires dual-source evidence; when text and gazetteer disagree, the node stays unlinked rather than mis-grounded.
\textbf{Scope.} The gold benchmark covers five Ming--Qing classical vernacular novels, and the cross-genre sample shares their strong plot-driven narrative mode; sparse-location psychological fiction is now covered (\S\ref{sec:cross-genre}). Generalization to other languages, genres, and contemporary fiction is untested. Main experiments use a single LLM family (Claude Sonnet 4); a DeepSeek V3 cross-check (\S\ref{sec:llm-baselines}) reproduces the structural pattern, but systematic cross-model evaluation remains open. Evaluation spans 90--122-chapter classical novels and, in the cross-genre sample, 1{,}395--2{,}451-chapter Chinese web serials (xianxia and steampunk-occult); our single-parent model does not distinguish physical containment from administrative jurisdiction. The synthetic contamination-free test (\S\ref{sec:contamination-free}) addresses the primary pretraining-memorization concern, but a human-authored post-2024 corpus would strengthen ecological validity. Future work: \begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}道\end{CJK}/\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}府\end{CJK}/\begin{CJK}{UTF8}{gbsn}郡\end{CJK} administrative-suffix extension, a human-authored post-2024 corpus, and a tightened ``proper place'' guideline followed by third-annotator replication.
